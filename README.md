# Clear-Tec-Balls

Cosmetic plugin for **Ballest of Them All** by CryT4x.
Adds transparent glass balls and a hat to the game.

Requires the **cosmetic-kit** plugin.

## Balls
- **Clear**: clear glass ball (light blue tint, 50% opacity)
- **Dots**: nearly transparent glass ball (20% opacity) with red metal dots at the top, bottom, left, right, front and back

## Hats
- **Coors**: glass hat with a bottom plate, a 45° indicator at the lower front and red metal dots at the top, bottom, left, right, front and back

## Settings
- **Hello-Message in Console**: shows or hides the CryT4x greeting in the console when the plugin starts (off by default)
